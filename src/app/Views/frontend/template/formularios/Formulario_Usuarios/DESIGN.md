## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-velocity, cinematic cyberpunk aesthetic designed for streaming, interactive media exploration, and advanced telemetry-style user identity management. 

### Core Ethos & Personality
- **Techno-futuristic & Immersive:** The interface resembles a high-tier executive deck from a sprawling megalopolis—dense with information yet razor-sharp in execution.
- **Electric Precision:** High-contrast luminous lines cut through abyssal shadows, directing the viewer's focus instantly to featured content and real-time interaction states.
- **Cinematic Noir:** Deep obsidian layers evoke midnight urban infrastructure, allowing vibrant movie key art and vivid neon vectors to rupture the dark plane with high dramatic intensity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for digital natives, cinema enthusiasts, and power users who favor sophisticated, boundary-pushing software over safe corporate sterility. The system evokes hyper-focus, high sensory engagement, and elevated technical capability.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising neon-on-obsidian dark space. Ambient dark backgrounds preserve OLED-depth, while concentrated electric tones articulate interactions and critical metadata.

### System Palette Roles
- **Base Obsidian (`#0a0a12`):** Primary canvas tone representing structural void; absorbs light and renders film stills with maximum dynamic range.
- **Deep Void / Surface Container (`#120e24`):** Secondary structural surface used for cards, floating HUD bars, and navigation drawers.
- **Neon Cyan (`#00f0ff`):** Primary interactive beacon. Applied to dominant action buttons, active navigation states, playback progress, and terminal-grade telemetry readouts.
- **Electric Ultraviolet (`#9d00ff`):** Secondary accent. Drives high-value features, streaming tier highlights, elevated user badges, and ambient atmospheric aura blurs.
- **Cyberpunk Hot Crimson (`#ff0055`):** Tertiary/Alert tone. Flags live broadcasts, destructive operations, system warnings, and exclusive movie premieres.
- **Surface Elevation Borders (`#00f0ff` at 20% to 40% alpha, `#9d00ff` at 30% alpha):** Structural grid outlines creating crisp, futuristic delineation.

## Typography

Typography balances futuristic geometric presence with developer-grade scanning precision.

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers an architectural, slightly angular modernist display tone. Headlines command immediate visual authority on desktop widescreen viewport scales.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (`Geist`):** Engineered for ultra-clean readability in dark environments, rendering technical synopses, user analytics, and platform controls without ocular fatigue.
- **HUD Micro-Labels & Badges (`Space Mono`):** Capitalized monospaced tags deliver hardware-level telemetry aesthetics for stream bitrates, audio channels, runtime stamps, and status diagnostics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is constructed on an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for widescreen media displays and dense diagnostic panels.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12-column layout with `margin-desktop` (4rem) and `gutter-desktop` (2rem). Content cards snap to strict ratios (16:9 for streaming hero units, 2:3 for vertical cinema posters). Left sidebar functions as a persistent HUD docking station.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout with fluid 1.5rem gutters. The navigation dock collapses to an icon rail or top floating capsule.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with compact 1rem gutters and 1.25rem margins. Poster feeds switch to single or two-column horizontally scrollable tracks to maintain visual fidelity.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm alternates between compact, high-density utility controls (e.g., telemetry bars, media metadata) separated by `space-xs` and `space-sm`, against expansive stage breaks (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`) that allow marquee content to breathe against the deep obsidian canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, organic drop shadows, relying instead on translucent glassmorphism, multi-stop neon edge illumination, and deep layer separation.

### Surface Tiers
- **Void Canvas (Layer 0):** Pure dark space (`#0a0a12`) hosting ambient background radial gradients tinted in saturated ultraviolet (`#9d00ff` at 8% opacity) and cyan (`#00f0ff` at 5% opacity).
- **Glass Panel (Layer 1):** Translucent `#120e24` at 70% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ultra-fine 1px border of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Active Overlay / Floating HUD (Layer 2):** `#1a1438` at 85% opacity with an intense localized perimeter glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2), inset 0 0 12px rgba(157, 0, 255, 0.15)`.
- **System Modals & Telemetry Overlays (Layer 3):** Solidified obsidian-violet with directional cyan corner brackets and a 1px border rendered in full `#00f0ff`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is sharp, tactical, and minimal. Pure roundness is avoided in favor of crisp technical forms.

### Corner Styling
- **Base Components:** Radius is strictly constrained to 0.25rem (`roundedness: 1`), keeping buttons, inputs, and system badges firmly anchored to an industrial aesthetic.
- **Large Panels & Player Shells:** Bounded at 0.5rem (`rounded-lg`) to balance screen framing without feeling bubbly.
- **HUD Chamfers (Optional Accent):** Key cards and primary status chips may implement a 45-degree corner clip (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to simulate military-grade digital avionics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber:** Neon Cyan background (`#00f0ff`), deep obsidian typography (`#0a0a12`), font `Space Mono` uppercase bold. Features an outer neon trace `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`. On hover, scale transforms by 1.02 with an expanded 24px cyan halo.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent surface with 1px border in `#9d00ff`, text rendered in `#00f0ff`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(157, 0, 255, 0.15)` with an inner neon glow.
- **Destructive HUD:** Outline border in `#ff0055`, crimson-tinted text with subtle alert pulsation.

### Media Cards & Posters
- **Streaming Card (16:9 & 2:3):** Wrapped in a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. On hover, border flashes to `#00f0ff`, media scales smoothly (1.05x), and a technical HUD overlay slides up displaying monospaced bitrate, audio codecs (Dolby Atmos, 4K HDR), and match percentages.
- **Hero Display Banner:** Edge-to-edge gradient integration fading into `#0a0a12` with floating quick-action pill clusters and dynamic live-stream indicator badges.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Filter Inputs:** Deep violet obsidian containers (`#120e24`) with inset 1px borders of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`. Focus state illuminates the border to `#00f0ff` with an outer soft cyan bloom. Cursor is a styled neon cyan block.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and octagonal perimeters. Checked states glow with solid `#00f0ff` cores and micro-monospaced indicators.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **HUD Chips:** Compact height (22px), `Space Mono` 10px uppercase, framed with 1px glowing neon border. Applied for content ratings (e.g., `[NC-17]`, `[4K UHD]`, `[ONLINE]`).

### Sliders & Playback Scrubber
- **Timeline Track:** 3px deep charcoal bar (`#1f1b36`). The elapsed buffer is an electric cyan gradient fading into ultraviolet, terminated by a sharp, glowing diamond-shaped playhead indicator.
